GAMES / USERS
  - Password-authenticated player accounts, unique room codes.
  - Singles 1-4 seats; teams 4/6/8 directly opposite; select same/opponent team.
  - Host-set countdown; empty seats automatically filled with bots, with cron fallback.
  - Choose from 48 marble colors plus custom hexadecimal; exact collisions rejected.
  - Entire deck, discard, private hands and game rules are server-controlled.
  - Dealer marker rotates whenever discarded cards are recycled.
  - Stats: games, wins, losses, bumps, assists; no client access to write stats.

GEOMETRY / LOCAL DEMO
  Imported geometry and royal artwork from angry-marbles-geometry-locked-arcade-v0.4.2.zip.
  pb_hooks/boardGeometry.cjs is the shared source for renderer and game-engine indices.
  Square singles; 4/6/8-player team polygons; 18 unique track holes per side.
  Red marker 2, green exit 8, five home holes, five visible base-rack holes per side.
  Browser-local demo allows mode/size selection, legal card moves, and discards without PocketBase.
  Local activity is simulated in one browser; online multiplayer is not yet verified.
  Original Jack, Queen, King, and Joker SVGs are under public/assets/cards/.
  GitHub Pages base is lowercase /angrymarbles/ and backend-free builds are supported.
